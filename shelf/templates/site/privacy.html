Who is responsible for your data

{{ d.entity }} ({{ d.entity_type }}), registered at {{ d.address }}, is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here. In the language of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you are the Data Principal — the person the data is about, and the person whose decisions about it we have to honour.

SHELF is the website; the assistant is the WhatsApp contact you message. They are one service run by one entity, and this policy covers both.

What we deliberately do not have

  • Your card, UPI ID or bank details. Those go to Razorpay and never reach us. We could not show them to you if you asked.
  • Your name, email, college, or address, unless you happen to type one into a message. We never ask for any of them.
  • Your location, contacts, or anything else on your phone. WhatsApp does not give it to us and we do not ask for it.
  • An advertising profile. We do not build one, we do not sell your data, and we do not use what you send to market anything to you or to anybody else.

What we do with it, and what we do not

Everything above exists to run the service you paid for: filing what you send, finding it again, and firing the reminders you asked for.

Your material is scoped to your number. Every query we run against it is filtered by your account, so one subscriber's search cannot reach another's files — that is enforced in the code and covered by tests, not left to care.

We do not use your files or messages to train any model, and we do not give them to anybody who would.

Data leaving India

The database and the application run in Singapore, and some of the companies above process data outside India. DPDP permits this except to countries the Central Government restricts, and we will stop using any provider that becomes restricted. If that matters to you, it is better to know before you subscribe than after.

Consent, and taking it back

We process your data because you asked us to, by subscribing and by sending the assistant things to keep. That consent covers the purposes in section 2 and nothing else.

You can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing it is the same action as asking for erasure — see section 6. Withdrawing consent ends the service, because there is no version of this product that works without holding what you send it. That is a consequence, not a penalty, and we would rather say it plainly here than surprise you later.

Your rights

DPDP gives you these. We answer all of them within {{ d.rights_response_days }} days, and usually far sooner.

A copy of everything

Ask and we send every row we hold about you, keyed by table, as a file. Not a summary and not a selection — the whole thing.

Correction and completion

If something we hold is wrong, tell us and we fix it. Most of it you can correct yourself by telling the assistant.

Erasure

Ask and we delete your account, every message, every deadline, every sign-in record, and every file — from the database and from file storage both. Files go first, deliberately: rows deleted while the file survives would leave your material sitting there with nothing pointing at it.

Erasure is done by a person, never by messaging the assistant. Everywhere else in this product a model proposes and a check decides, which suits something that can be undone. A complete deletion cannot be undone, so it is kept off any path a misread message could reach.

Nominating someone

You can nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapable of exercising them yourself. Write to the Grievance Officer and we will record it.

Grievance redressal

{{ d.grievance_officer }} is the Grievance Officer. Write to {{ d.grievance_email }} and you get an acknowledgement and an answer within {{ d.rights_response_days }} days. If we do not resolve it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Going to us first is not a requirement we can impose on you, but it is usually faster.

Deletion when you stop paying

If your subscription lapses we keep everything for 90 days and then delete it automatically — account, messages, deadlines and files. The 90 days exist so a failed card or a late renewal costs you nothing.

After that it is gone, and we cannot restore it. Keep your own copies of anything you would be upset to lose. This is a filing service, not a backup.

How it is protected

  • Everything travels over encrypted connections.
  • Our logs record identifiers, never message content or phone numbers. Reading our own logs does not show anyone what you sent. Database errors are configured not to echo the values that caused them, because one of those values is your message.
  • Operational alerts carry an account identifier and nothing else, so a third-party alerting channel never receives your material.
  • Signing in to this website is a one-time link the assistant sends you on WhatsApp. It works once, expires in minutes, and can be withdrawn — we never send a code to your phone that someone could talk you into forwarding.
  • Access to the production database is limited to the people operating the service.

If there is a breach

DPDP requires us to notify you and the Data Protection Board if your data is breached. We will, without waiting to establish blame, and we will tell you what was affected and what to do about it.

Being straight about this: the written internal procedure for handling a breach is not finished. The obligation binds us regardless, and this sentence is here so the gap is visible to us rather than only to a regulator.

People under 18

This service is intended for people aged 18 and over, and by subscribing you confirm you are.

DPDP treats anyone under 18 as a child and requires verifiable parental consent before processing their data. A first-year college student in India is routinely 17. We do not currently ask, and a statement of intent is not the same as a control.

If you are under 18, do not subscribe. If you have, tell us at {{ d.grievance_email }} and we will refund you in full and delete everything.

Changes

If this policy changes in substance we will tell you on WhatsApp before it takes effect, and the date at the top of this page will change. We will not quietly widen what we do with your data and rely on you re-reading a page.

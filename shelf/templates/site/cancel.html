Why it happens there and not here

Your subscription is tied to your phone number, and that number is the only identity this service has. A number typed into a web form proves nothing about who typed it — a page like this could cancel a stranger's subscription for them. A message from the phone itself cannot.

The same reasoning is why signing in to your files starts with a link the assistant sends you, rather than a password.

What happens next

  • No further charges, from that moment.
  • Full access until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Nothing you have stored is deleted. It waits 90 days in case you come back, then goes automatically.
  • Want it gone sooner? Email {{ d.support_email }} and ask — we delete everything, from the database and from file storage both.

The full terms are on the cancellation and refunds page, including when a payment comes back.

If the message does not work

Email {{ d.support_email }} with your number. We cancel it by hand the same working day and confirm by reply. If you have lost access to the number itself, say so — we will verify another way rather than leave you paying for something you cannot reach.

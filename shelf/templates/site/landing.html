{# The landing page. Standalone on purpose: it does not extend site/base.html, because the direction gives it a fixed half-screen stage and no top logo -- the giant wordmark *is* the logo. Every other site page keeps base.html. Direction: "split stage" (tasteskill, seed 262542071). Half the screen never moves and holds the object; the other half scrolls and carries the argument. The idea: the logomark is already two extruded isometric slabs -- a shelf. So the fixed half is that mark, built for real in 3D, and it performs the product: the class group's forwarded chaos falls in, gets caught, and files itself onto Material, PYQ and Info. The last quarter folds the shelves into the exact mark on the browser tab. Why no Three.js: the mark is *isometric*, not perspective, so the projection is two lines of arithmetic. A WebGL library would be ~600KB on a cold load, paid for by a student on mobile data, to do less faithfully what a 2D canvas does exactly. The renderer is below in full and depends on nothing. Everything reads and works with JavaScript off; the canvas is the only part that needs it, and its absence leaves the page whole. #} SHELF — forward it once, ask for it whenever

Questions

Do I have to install anything? No. It is one more contact in the WhatsApp you already have open. The website is for browsing and is optional.

How does it know which subject? From what you say when you send it. If your words fit two subjects, it asks instead of guessing.

What if I send forty files at once? That is one reply, not forty. Messages arriving together are treated as a single turn.

Can it read a scan? It reads the caption you send with it, and it reads photographs: a timetable screenshot becomes your subject list.

What about .zip files? WhatsApp never delivers those to us, so nothing can store them. Send the files inside, and it will tell you so if you try.

Who sees my notes? Nobody here reads them. Logs carry identifiers only: never message text, never your number.

Is my number safe? You prove it by sending us a code from your own phone. Nothing is ever sent to you, so there is nothing to be talked out of.

What if I stop paying? You keep what you paid for until that period ends. After that access closes, and the data is deleted on the schedule the privacy policy states.

Can I get everything back out? Ask, and it sends a copy of all of it. Leaving should cost nothing.

Does it work on a cheap phone? Yes. The chat is WhatsApp, and the website is text and pictures with nothing to install.

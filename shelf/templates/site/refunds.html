The short version

Cancel whenever you like — it stops the next charge and you keep what you already paid for. New subscribers get a full refund within {{ d.refund_window_days }} days of the first payment, no reason needed. Anything we charged in error comes back in full, always.

The rest of this page is the same thing said precisely.

Cancelling

Send "cancel my subscription" to the assistant on WhatsApp. It stops the recurring mandate immediately and confirms in the chat. There is no form to find and no retention script to sit through.

Cancelling from the chat rather than from a web page is deliberate: your subscription is tied to your phone, and a number typed into a form proves nothing about who typed it. A message from the phone itself does.

If that fails for any reason, email {{ d.support_email }} with your number and we will cancel it by hand the same working day. See the cancellation page.

What cancelling does and does not do

  • It stops all future charges.
  • You keep full access until the end of the period already paid for.
  • Nothing you have stored is deleted because you cancelled. It is kept for 90 days in case you come back, then deleted automatically. Ask us and we will delete it sooner.

Refunds

First subscription, within {{ d.refund_window_days }} days

Full refund of that payment, for any reason or none. Email {{ d.support_email }} from or mentioning your registered number. We do not ask why.

After that

Payments already taken are not refunded, because the month they cover has been provided. You are never charged for a month you did not want: cancelling stops the next charge, and a monthly plan means at most one month is ever at stake.

Always refunded in full, whenever it happened

  • Charged after you cancelled.
  • Charged more than once for the same month.
  • Charged on a number that was never activated, or where the service never worked for you and we could not fix it.
  • Charged by someone who is not you, on a number you did not authorise. Tell us and we will refund it and close the account.
  • You were under 18 when you subscribed. Full refund, everything deleted, no argument.
  • We ended your account for a reason other than your breach of the terms — the unused part of the period comes back.

How to ask

Email {{ d.support_email }} with the number the subscription is on and, if you have it, the Razorpay payment reference from your receipt. That is all we need — you do not have to explain yourself.

We acknowledge within 48 hours and decide within 5 working days.

How long a refund takes

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method — we cannot send one anywhere else, and we would not be able to even if you asked.

  • We issue it within 5 working days of approving it.
  • Razorpay and your bank then take their own time, typically 5 to 7 working days, sometimes up to one billing cycle for a card.

If it has not arrived within 10 working days of our confirmation, tell us and we will chase Razorpay with the reference on your behalf.

If you disagree with a decision

Write to {{ d.grievance_officer }} at {{ d.grievance_email }}, who will look at it independently of whoever answered first. If you are still unhappy, you may take it to a Consumer Commission. Raising a chargeback with your bank is your right and we will not penalise you for it, but telling us first is almost always faster.
